# PodClip downloads

The signed and notarized PodClip installers, handed out by the download buttons on [podclip.studio](https://podclip.studio).
Get them from your account there: the apps ask you to sign in, and the editor extensions need a plan that includes them.

- **PodClip for Mac**: Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later
- **PodClip Text for Final Cut Pro**: the captions and 3D text extension (needs PodClip for Mac)
- **PodClip for Premiere Pro**: the captions and 3D text panel (needs PodClip for Mac)
